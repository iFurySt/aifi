# Company News Source Policy

Use this guide when collecting news and information-side signals.

## Source Priority

1. Company investor-relations press releases, official blogs, regulatory
   releases, and executive statements.
2. Filings and exchange announcements.
3. Major financial media with dated bylines.
4. Sector or trade publications with domain expertise.
5. Analyst notes, expert commentary, podcasts, and newsletters.
6. Social media, forums, and unsourced rumor channels.

Lower-priority sources can be useful for narrative discovery, but they should not
override primary evidence.

## Event Types

Track these categories:

- earnings preannouncements or guidance changes
- major product launches, delays, recalls, or roadmap changes
- executive changes and board actions
- customer wins, losses, or contract changes
- regulatory approvals, investigations, subsidies, sanctions, or litigation
- M&A, divestitures, restructuring, layoffs, or capex changes
- supply-chain constraints or capacity changes
- notable analyst upgrades, downgrades, or estimate revisions
- major price-moving rumors, clearly labeled as unconfirmed

## Freshness Rules

- For "recent" requests, state the date range used.
- For "latest" requests, browse or query current sources before answering.
- If current retrieval fails, write the failure into the digest and do not imply
  current coverage.
- If old news is still important, label it as background rather than recent.
