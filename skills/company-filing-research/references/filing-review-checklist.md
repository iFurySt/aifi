# Filing Review Checklist

Use this checklist to avoid shallow filing summaries.

## Filing Inventory

Record:

- filing type
- filing date
- period covered
- accession or filing identifier when available
- source URL
- raw file path, if archived

## Sections To Review

For annual and quarterly reports:

- business overview and segment changes
- management discussion and analysis
- revenue, gross margin, operating margin, free cash flow, and capex commentary
- liquidity, debt maturities, covenants, and credit facilities
- customer concentration and supplier dependency
- backlog, bookings, inventory, and demand signals when disclosed
- risk factors and changed wording
- legal proceedings
- controls and procedures
- related-party transactions

For current reports:

- event type and date
- attached exhibits
- earnings release or presentation
- guidance change
- executive or board change
- material agreement
- restructuring, impairment, acquisition, or divestiture

For proxy statements:

- board composition
- executive compensation incentives
- shareholder proposals
- related-party issues
- governance changes

## Change Detection

When comparing filings, focus on substantive changes rather than formatting:

- new risk factors
- removed or softened risks
- changes in demand language
- changes in customer or supplier dependency
- new debt or liquidity language
- litigation updates
- segment reclassification
- accounting policy changes
