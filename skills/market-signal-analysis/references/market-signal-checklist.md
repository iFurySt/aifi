# Market Signal Checklist

## Data To Consider

| Signal | Use | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price return | Measures market reaction over a window | Needs benchmark comparison. |
| Relative return | Separates company signal from sector or market beta | Choose relevant peers. |
| Volume | Flags unusual attention or liquidity | High volume does not imply direction. |
| Volatility | Indicates uncertainty or event risk | Use consistent window. |
| Drawdown | Shows downside path and sentiment damage | Context matters. |
| Valuation multiples | Compares expectations and fundamentals | Requires fresh price and clean financials. |
| Short interest | Can indicate skepticism or crowded trade | Often delayed. |
| Options activity | Can show event positioning | Noisy and often speculative. |
| Analyst revisions | Captures market expectation changes | Separate from price action. |

## Benchmark Choices

- Broad US market: S&P 500 or Nasdaq Composite.
- Semiconductor target: SOX index, semiconductor ETF, or relevant peers.
- Mega-cap technology: Nasdaq 100 plus direct peers.
- Non-US target: local market index and local sector benchmark.

## Interpretation Rules

- Price confirms that expectations changed, not why they changed.
- A large move after earnings may reflect guidance, positioning, or macro
  factors; check event evidence before assigning cause.
- If the target has multiple share classes or listings, specify which instrument
  was analyzed.
