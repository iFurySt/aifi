# Financial Metric Map

Choose metrics based on the target's business model. Do not collect everything
if it will not inform the research question.

## Core Metrics

| Area | Metrics | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | revenue, segment revenue, year-over-year growth, sequential growth | Segment trends often matter more than consolidated growth. |
| Profitability | gross margin, operating margin, net margin, EBITDA margin | Label GAAP vs adjusted. |
| Cash flow | operating cash flow, free cash flow, capex, FCF margin | Define free cash flow formula used. |
| Balance sheet | cash, debt, net debt, current ratio, debt maturities | Note restricted cash or unusual financing. |
| Capital allocation | buybacks, dividends, M&A, capex plans | Tie to cash generation. |
| Valuation | market cap, enterprise value, P/E, EV/sales, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield | Requires fresh market data. |
| Guidance | company guidance, consensus estimates, estimate revisions | Keep separate from actuals. |

## Semiconductor And Intel-Relevant Metrics

Use when the target is a chip company, foundry, or semiconductor equipment name:

- data center revenue
- client/PC revenue
- foundry revenue and operating loss
- gross margin recovery
- capex intensity
- inventory days
- wafer capacity or utilization when disclosed
- process-node roadmap milestones
- CHIPS Act or subsidy-related funding
- AI accelerator product traction
- competitor share signals

## Data Handling Rules

- Use fiscal period labels from the company source.
- When calculating growth, show the denominator or period compared.
- If a metric is estimated from a chart or secondary source, label it.
- Keep downloaded spreadsheets or PDFs in the archive when they are material.
