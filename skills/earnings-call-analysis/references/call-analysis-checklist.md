# Earnings Call Analysis Checklist

## Materials To Collect

- earnings press release
- investor presentation
- conference call transcript
- webcast replay notes or official recording link
- non-GAAP reconciliation
- guidance table
- prior-period call or presentation for comparison

## Prepared Remarks

Extract:

- headline performance claims
- segment commentary
- demand and pricing language
- margin drivers
- capex and capacity plans
- product roadmap claims
- customer or end-market commentary
- restructuring or cost-control plans
- guidance and assumptions

## Analyst Q&A

Track:

- questions management avoided
- repeated analyst concerns
- questions about margins, competition, inventory, pricing, and guidance
- management answers that narrow or expand prior claims
- evidence of confidence, defensiveness, or uncertainty

## Language Shift Patterns

Flag changes such as:

- "strong demand" becoming "stable demand"
- "on track" becoming "making progress"
- "temporary" headwinds persisting across quarters
- new caveats around customer adoption or product timing
- margin recovery pushed into later periods
