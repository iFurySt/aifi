## Synthesis Rules

- Use evidence links, not vague phrases like "reports say".
- If all evidence comes from one source type, weaken confidence.
- Keep a separate section for contradictions instead of smoothing them away.
- Avoid target prices unless the workflow has a dedicated valuation model and
  source assumptions.
- Treat analyst sentiment as one input, not the conclusion.
