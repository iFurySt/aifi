## Market Interpretation

The first-day move implies public-market demand far exceeded the final IPO allocation price. That enthusiasm is understandable given AI infrastructure scarcity and the OpenAI/AWS narrative, but it also creates a valuation hurdle: at the first-day close, the stock was roughly 68% above an already upsized $185 IPO price. Near-term trading may be driven more by float scarcity, IPO momentum, and AI theme demand than by new operating evidence.
