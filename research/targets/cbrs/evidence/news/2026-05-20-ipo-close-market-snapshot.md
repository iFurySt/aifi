## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- The main incremental change is not a new operating milestone; it is company and SEC confirmation that the IPO closed with the full greenshoe exercised and that the stock continued to trade at a large premium to the offering price.
- The higher final proceeds modestly improve the near-term capital cushion for a capital-intensive cloud and data-center buildout, but they do not resolve the core execution questions around OpenAI/AWS capacity delivery, revenue diversification, and normalized margins.
- With only a few trading sessions available, price action should be treated as float scarcity, AI hardware demand, and IPO momentum more than a validated change in business fundamentals.
- The existing thesis still depends on conversion of OpenAI/AWS-related obligations into revenue, customer diversification, and normalized cloud economics after capacity ramps.
