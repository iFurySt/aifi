## Transformations

- Q1 2026 growth bars use Nokia-reported percentages. Comparable constant-currency / portfolio growth is not mixed with reported growth without labels.
- Q1 2026 segment revenue bars use EUR B values from the archived financial snapshot. Sub-units overlap with segment totals and are labeled as non-additive.
- Valuation range uses StockAnalysis enterprise value of USD 70.07B, Nokia 2026 comparable operating profit guidance of EUR 2.0-2.5B, Nokia 2028 comparable operating profit target of EUR 2.7-3.2B, and an EUR/USD assumption of 1.15 from the Q1 2026 outlook context.

- No live market data, peer valuation table, DCF, or historical price CSV is embedded.

- Charts are static and optimized for browser readability, not model recalculation.
