## Narrative Changes

- Nokia is no longer best framed as a consumer phone story. The investable story is B2B network infrastructure: optical transport, IP networking, radio/core networks, technology standards licensing, and selective AI/defense/enterprise use cases.
- The key positive narrative is that AI/cloud traffic could turn optical and IP networking from cyclical telecom capex into a more durable data-center-adjacent growth engine.
- The key skeptical narrative is that current market enthusiasm may already discount much of the 2026-2028 improvement before Nokia proves sustained revenue growth and reported-profit conversion.

## Confirmed Facts

- Nokia now reports under a simplified 2026 structure built around Network Infrastructure, Mobile Infrastructure, and Portfolio Businesses.
- Nokia completed the Infinera acquisition in 2025 and continues to target more than EUR 200 million of net comparable operating profit synergies by 2027.
- NVIDIA made a USD 1 billion equity investment in Nokia as part of a strategic AI-RAN partnership.
- Q1 2026 AI & Cloud customer sales grew 49% year over year on a constant currency and portfolio basis.

## Unconfirmed Or Disputed Claims

- The size, timing, and profitability of AI-RAN revenue remain unproven from retrieved public sources.
- It is not yet clear whether agentic AI broadband features materially change Nokia's consolidated growth rate or are mainly defensive/product-enhancement work.
