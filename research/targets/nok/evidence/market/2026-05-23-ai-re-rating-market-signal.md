- The 2026-05-22 price action makes Nokia more urgent to monitor. The stock is
  no longer only a low-multiple telecom-equipment recovery; market participants
  are increasingly pricing an AI infrastructure / data-center networking story.
- The risk/reward balance has changed. A higher multiple can be justified if
  Nokia converts AI networking, optical, AI-RAN, and federal / broadband

- This market signal is notification-worthy because it combines a large price
