## Balance Sheet And Cash Flow

- Nokia had EUR 3.8B of net cash and interest-bearing financial investments at Q1 2026.
- Q1 2026 free cash flow was EUR 0.6B, but management connected this to working-capital seasonality, so it should not be annualized mechanically.
- FY 2025 free cash flow of EUR 1.5B represented 72% conversion of comparable operating profit, a strong cash result relative to comparable profit.
- Reported FY 2025 operating profit was only EUR 885M versus EUR 2.024B comparable operating profit; this difference matters because recurring restructuring, integration, and portfolio costs can dilute shareholder economics.

## Valuation Context

- StockAnalysis showed Nokia at USD 13.17 at the 2026-05-12 close, USD 72.90B market cap, and USD 70.07B enterprise value.
- Using Nokia's 2026 comparable operating profit outlook of EUR 2.0-2.5B and the Q1 outlook FX assumption of EUR/USD 1.15, the USD 70.07B EV implies roughly 24x-30x 2026 comparable operating profit. This is a simple inference, not company guidance.
- Using Nokia's 2028 comparable operating profit target of EUR 2.7-3.2B and the same 1.15 FX for a rough apples-to-apples view, the same EV implies roughly 19x-23x 2028 target comparable operating profit before discounting execution risk. This is also an inference.
- The valuation has become sensitive to whether Nokia is viewed as a high-quality AI/cloud networking compounder or as a cyclical telecom-equipment vendor with temporary AI optionality.

## Guidance And Revisions

- Nokia's Q1 2026 report maintained full-year 2026 comparable operating profit guidance of EUR 2.0-2.5B.
- Nokia said it was tracking somewhat above the midpoint of that range after Q1.
- Nokia raised/updated 2026 Network Infrastructure assumptions to 12-14% net sales growth, with combined IP and Optical Networks expected to grow 18-20%.
- Nokia expects 2026 capex of EUR 900M-1.0B, driven partly by additional Optical Networks manufacturing capacity.
- Nokia targets 2028 comparable operating profit of EUR 2.7-3.2B, Network Infrastructure operating margin of 13-17%, and free cash flow conversion of 65-75%.
