## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- The consensus update is the more useful incremental data point. Nokia's 2026 guide midpoint is EUR 2.25B of comparable operating profit, while the post-Q1 consensus average is EUR 2.364B.
- Nokia's AI narrative is broadening from optical/data-center transport into operational automation for fixed networks. That helps product positioning but should not be treated as a near-term earnings driver without order or revenue disclosures.
- The existing valuation concern remains: after the May rerating, Nokia needs sustained AI/cloud and optical execution to justify being priced more like an AI infrastructure beneficiary than a slow-growth telecom equipment company.
