## Balance Sheet And Cash Flow

- Intel is not liquidity-constrained today, but it is capital-intensive and leverage-sensitive.
- The Fab 34 buyout is strategically accretive if the fab ramps well, but it consumes liquidity and adds refinancing risk through a $6.5B term loan.
- Adjusted FCF remains negative in Q1. Management expects positive adjusted FCF for full-year 2026 excluding the Fab 34 buyout, but that is still a guide, not achieved history.

## Valuation Context

- Current market data from the finance tool: INTC at $124.92, market cap about $635B, latest trade 2026-05-09 00:15 UTC.
- At this market cap, near-term earnings power does not support the valuation by itself. The stock is being valued on a successful multi-year turnaround: stronger DCAI, 18A/14A execution, foundry customers, and strategic scarcity as US leading-edge capacity.
- Q1 non-GAAP net income of $1.485B annualizes to roughly $5.9B. That is a rough heuristic, not a model, but it shows why the current price requires large future improvement.

## Guidance And Revisions

- Q2 2026 revenue guide: $13.8B-$14.8B.
- Q2 non-GAAP gross margin guide: 39.0%, down from Q1's 41.0%.
- Q2 non-GAAP EPS guide: $0.20, below Q1's $0.29.
- Management expects DCAI up double digits sequentially in Q2 and CCG sequential growth, offset by PC weakness later in the year.
