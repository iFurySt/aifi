## Key Disclosures

- Q1 2026 revenue was $13.577B versus $12.667B in Q1 2025; gross margin was 39.4% versus 36.9%.
- GAAP operating loss was $3.136B, mainly due to restructuring and other charges of $4.070B, including Mobileye goodwill impairment.
- Intel said Q1 revenue was limited by internal and external supply constraints and expected those constraints to persist throughout 2026.
- Intel had cash and short-term investments of $32.789B and total debt of $45.031B at 2026-03-28.
- Intel repurchased Apollo's 49% minority interest in Ireland SCIP/Fab 34 in April 2026 for approximately $14.2B, funded with cash, short-term investments, and a $6.5B term loan.

## Changes From Prior Period

- Reported Q1 revenue rose 7.2% YoY, driven primarily by Intel Products and DCAI.
- Total R&D and MG&A expenses fell 8% YoY, helped by restructuring and Altera deconsolidation.
- Intel's cash plus short-term investments declined from $37.416B at 2025 year-end to $32.789B at Q1 2026.
- Total debt declined from $46.585B to $45.031B at Q1 end, but the April Fab 34 financing adds a new $6.5B term loan after quarter-end.

## Risk Factor Changes And Carryover Risks

- The foundry risk remains central. Intel's 2025 annual report disclosed that failure to secure a significant external 14A customer could cause Intel to pause or discontinue 14A and successor leading-edge node development.
- The 10-Q reinforces supply-chain and manufacturing-execution risk: supply constraints are expected to persist through 2026, and shortages of substrates, memory, and other components could further limit demand fulfillment.
- Intel's capital structure is improving operationally but still leveraged: Fab 34 creates more ownership upside but uses cash and adds near-term debt/refinancing risk.
- GAAP losses are not just "fake"; some charges are non-cash, but foundry losses and capex/cash-flow burden are real.

## Financial Handoffs

- Model Q1 on two layers: operating recovery in Products/DCAI and continuing Foundry cash/economic drag.
- Adjusted FCF and capex matter more than headline non-GAAP EPS for sizing.
- Track whether cash balance after Fab 34 buyout remains comfortable relative to debt maturities and capex.

## Open Questions

- How much of Q1 gross-margin upside came from non-recurring inventory benefits versus durable yield/mix gains?
- How much of Q2/Q3 revenue is constrained by supply versus true demand?
- Will 14A get an external customer with enough committed volume and economics to de-risk the node?
