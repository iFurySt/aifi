## Headline Results

- Revenue: $13.6B, up 7% YoY.
- Non-GAAP gross margin: 41.0%, up 1.8 percentage points YoY.
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 12.3%, up 6.9 percentage points YoY.
- Non-GAAP EPS: $0.29, versus $0.13 in Q1 2025 and above company guidance.
- GAAP EPS: $(0.73), burdened by restructuring, Mobileye impairment, and other items.
- Operating cash flow: $1.1B; adjusted free cash flow: $(2.0)B.

## Management Message

- Management's core message: AI inference/agentic workloads are increasing demand for CPUs and advanced packaging, not only GPUs.
- CEO Lip-Bu Tan argued that the CPU is reasserting itself as the orchestration/control layer for AI production workloads.
- Management said Xeon demand is strong and sustained, and that demand is ahead of supply.
- Intel said 18A yields are ahead of internal projections and that 14A customer evaluations are active.
- The company expects early 14A design commitments beginning in 2H 2026 and into 1H 2027.

## Guidance And Assumptions

- Q2 2026 revenue guide: $13.8B-$14.8B.
- Q2 non-GAAP EPS guide: $0.20.
- Q2 non-GAAP gross margin guide: 39.0%, lower than Q1 because of a larger contribution from early-ramp Intel 18A and non-repeat Q1 inventory benefits.
- Management expects PC demand to weaken in 2H 2026, with full-year PC unit TAM down low double digits.
- Management expects server CPU industry unit growth to be double digit in 2026, with momentum extending into 2027.
- Capex is now expected to be roughly flat to last year, not flat-to-down.

## Analyst Q&A Pressure Points

- Full Q&A transcript was not archived in this run.
- Based on prepared remarks and filings, the natural pressure points are 18A gross margin, 14A customer commitments, Foundry losses, PC demand weakening, Fab 34 debt/refinancing, and whether AI CPU demand is durable.

## Language Shifts

- Intel is no longer speaking only defensively about survival; management frames the opportunity around supply scarcity, CPU demand, 18A progress, 14A evaluations, and advanced packaging.
- Important caveat: management still calls foundry a long journey and explicitly acknowledges macro, geopolitical, component-cost, PC-demand, and early-ramp margin risks.

## Handoffs

- Financials: Reconcile non-GAAP operating recovery with negative FCF and GAAP loss.
- Risks: Monitor whether 18A/14A claims become customer commitments and margin improvement.
- Thesis: Treat Q1 as real evidence of recovery, but Apple/Terafab as unproven upside.
