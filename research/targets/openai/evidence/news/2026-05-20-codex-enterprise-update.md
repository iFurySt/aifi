## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- Codex is becoming a central enterprise wedge, not only a developer feature. The Dell partnership extends Codex toward controlled environments where large customers may be reluctant to send data to public SaaS or cloud-only tooling.

- For listed readthroughs, Dell may get a differentiated AI Factory narrative, while Microsoft/AWS remain relevant distribution channels. The OpenAI thesis remains indirect for public-market investors.
