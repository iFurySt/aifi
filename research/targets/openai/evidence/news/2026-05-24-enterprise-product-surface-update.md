## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- This update strengthens the enterprise-control side of the OpenAI thesis.
  The prior local Codex update already captured Appshots, Goal mode, browser
  annotations, locked computer use, mobile remote access, access tokens, and
  Dell hybrid / on-prem distribution. The newly archived items add the
  management layer: admin analytics, plugin sharing defaults, EKM workspace
  agents, and Intune-managed mobile access.
- The pattern is commercially important because large customers usually need
  observability, policy controls, version history, device management, and
  repeatable internal workflows before agent usage can move from pilots to
  scaled deployment.
- The update is not a standalone revenue disclosure. Watch for conversion from
  richer enterprise controls into paid seats, higher Codex usage, add-on
  credits, workspace-agent adoption, or retention metrics.
- The governance burden also rises: local plugins, scheduled agents, Slack
  workflows, spreadsheets, files, and mobile access increase the need for audit,
  permissioning, data-boundary, and endpoint-security controls.

## Watch Items

- Whether OpenAI discloses Codex active users, enterprise attach rate, credits
  consumed, accepted-code metrics, or expansion from pilot to production.
- Whether plugin sharing becomes broadly enabled for Enterprise workspaces and
  whether customers publish reusable internal plugin patterns.
- Whether EKM workspace agents unlock adoption in regulated customers that were
  previously blocked by key-management requirements.
- Whether ChatGPT for Excel / Google Sheets creates measurable usage beyond
  one-off productivity experiments.
