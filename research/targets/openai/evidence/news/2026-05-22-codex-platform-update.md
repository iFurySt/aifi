- The update complements the already archived Dell hybrid / on-prem enterprise
  Codex distribution and Enterprise/Edu access-token surfaces.

  tokens, desktop apps, and connectors increase utility but also increase
  scrutiny around permissioning, audit logs, data handling, and endpoint
  security.
