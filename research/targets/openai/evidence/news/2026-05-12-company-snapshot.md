## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- OpenAI is now both a frontier-model lab and a large-scale distribution company: ChatGPT, APIs, enterprise deployments, and ads each map to different margin and risk profiles.
- The funding scale suggests the company is optimizing for infrastructure capacity and strategic endurance, not near-term capital efficiency.
- AWS distribution reduces single-platform dependence and expands enterprise procurement paths, but it also makes OpenAI's cloud economics more visible and contested.
- Ads could materially expand consumer monetization, but it introduces trust, ranking, and product-quality risks.
