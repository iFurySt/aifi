## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- This update does not weaken the OpenAI growth thesis by itself, but it raises
  the priority of security diligence around Codex, desktop apps, connectors, and
  enterprise data access.
- The sensitive-conversation update is strategically consistent with OpenAI's
  push into personal and regulated-adjacent user surfaces. Better safety
  behavior can reduce adoption friction, but failures would be reputationally
  expensive.
- The right model is "trust infrastructure investment," not near-term revenue.
  These updates should be tracked alongside C2PA / SynthID provenance, Teams
  sync, personal finance, Codex enterprise access, and enterprise admin controls.
