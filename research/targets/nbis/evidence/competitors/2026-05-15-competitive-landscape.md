# Nebius Competitive Landscape

## Positioning

Nebius is best classified as an AI-native infrastructure cloud or neocloud. It is not primarily a semiconductor company. Its competitive edge, if it exists, should show up in four areas:

- Faster access to large-scale NVIDIA systems.
- Better AI workload operations than general-purpose cloud.
- Lower friction for training, fine-tuning, inference, storage, and managed services.
- Financing and construction execution that turns contracted demand into usable capacity.

## Competitor Map

| Competitor type | Examples | Advantage versus Nebius | Nebius counter-position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hyperscalers | Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle OCI | Balance sheet, existing enterprise relationships, global data-center footprint, platform bundling. | Purpose-built AI cloud, faster specialized capacity deployment, willingness to serve other AI builders and hyperscalers. |
| Neoclouds | CoreWeave, Lambda, Crusoe | Similar NVIDIA-based model; some peers have earlier scale in certain customer segments. | Large Microsoft/Meta contracts, NVIDIA strategic investment, and full-stack AI platform push. |
| NVIDIA ecosystem | NVIDIA DGX Cloud, partner clouds | NVIDIA controls the core accelerator roadmap and ecosystem economics. | Nebius is a deployment and operations partner rather than a chip challenger; it can benefit from NVIDIA-led demand. |
| Custom silicon stacks | Google TPU, AWS Trainium/Inferentia, Microsoft Maia, Cerebras | Lower long-term unit cost if workloads internalize onto proprietary chips. | Customers still need near-term NVIDIA capacity, multi-cloud optionality, and flexible external capacity. |
| Traditional data-center / colocation | Equinix, Digital Realty, hyperscale build-to-suit providers | Real estate, power procurement, and operating scale. | Nebius adds GPU systems, AI software, customer contracts, and workload operations on top of data-center capacity. |

## Differentiation Tests

The investment case should not stop at "AI demand is high." Nebius needs proof in these dimensions:

1. Capacity delivery: connected MW, active MW, GPU count, and customer go-live dates.
2. Utilization: sustained usage on capacity not dedicated to Microsoft/Meta.
3. Unit economics: gross margin and adjusted EBITDA after depreciation and financing costs are visible.
4. Software attach: Token Factory and managed inference should increase retention or margin, not just create marketing language.
5. Customer mix: more enterprise and AI-native customers outside a few mega-contracts.

## NVIDIA Relationship

NVIDIA is both a tailwind and a dependency. The March 2026 partnership and $2B investment are strong validation signals. They may improve early access, system design support, fleet operations, and customer credibility. But the 20-F risk factors make the other side clear: Nebius currently relies on NVIDIA for the GPUs it uses. If supply, pricing, export controls, or customer preferences shift, Nebius has limited room to substitute without disrupting commitments.

## Practical Comparison to Cerebras

Cerebras tries to change the compute substrate. Nebius tries to own the operating layer around the dominant compute substrate. That makes Nebius easier to underwrite on near-term revenue and harder to underwrite on long-term moat: if NVIDIA GPU supply becomes abundant and hyperscalers absorb more demand internally, Nebius must prove operational and software differentiation.
