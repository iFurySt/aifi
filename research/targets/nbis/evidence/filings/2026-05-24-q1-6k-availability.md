## Investment-Relevant Readthrough

- The incremental filing signal is not a new operating milestone; it is a
  provenance upgrade. The Q1 numbers are now backed by a filed 6-K with
  operating review and unaudited financial statements.
- The 6-K reinforces the main NBIS diligence issue: growth is real and customer
  prepayments are meaningful, but the model is capital intensive and depends on
  converting advance-funded capacity into durable, high-utilization revenue.
- Because the filing confirms $3.198B of customer advances as the main driver
  of Q1 operating cash flow, future updates should separate cash inflow from
  underlying free cash flow economics.

## Watch Items

- Any later 6-K or press release disclosing debt, asset-backed financing,
  customer prepayments, or capex funding.
- Whether deferred revenue converts into recognized revenue without margin
  degradation or service-level penalties.
- Any segment disclosure that separates Nebius AI cloud utilization and margins
  from Avride / TripleTen / investment gains.
