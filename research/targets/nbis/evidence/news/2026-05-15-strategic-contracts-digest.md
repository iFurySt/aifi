## Interpretation

Nebius has crossed the line from "GPU cloud startup" into "contracted AI infrastructure buildout platform." The company has named blue-chip customers and partners: Microsoft, Meta, and NVIDIA. That is a materially stronger commercial signal than generic AI demand commentary.

The important nuance is that these agreements are not free optionality. They convert commercial demand into an obligation to finance, procure, deploy, power, and operate large GPU fleets. The Microsoft 6-K explicitly ties obligations to financing confirmation, and the 20-F highlights late delivery, underutilization, and supplier risks. The Meta contract is especially large because it creates both committed dedicated capacity and optional/available capacity mechanics.

## Cerebras Comparison

- Cerebras is an architectural bet: can wafer-scale systems beat or meaningfully complement GPU clusters for inference and training economics?
- Nebius is an execution and financing bet: can a specialized operator deploy NVIDIA-based AI factories faster and more efficiently than customers and hyperscalers can do themselves?
- Cerebras competes more directly with NVIDIA silicon in selected workloads; Nebius monetizes NVIDIA scarcity and engineering support.

## Watch Items

- Delivery milestones for Microsoft tranches in 2025-2026 and Meta capacity starting early 2027.
- Whether customer prepayments lower external financing needs or merely pull forward obligations.
- Whether NVIDIA supply access remains preferential as GPU demand broadens.
- Whether Token Factory, Eigen AI, Clarifai IP, and Tavily improve margins or mostly add acquisition integration risk.
