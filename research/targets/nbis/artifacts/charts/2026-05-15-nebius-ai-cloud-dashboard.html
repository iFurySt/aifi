Research Read

Nebius is a stronger near-term operating story than a pure architecture bet: it already has named hyperscaler customers, visible revenue growth, and NVIDIA ecosystem validation. The open question is whether gigawatt-scale AI factories can be financed, built, powered, refreshed, and filled at attractive owner economics after depreciation, interest expense, and future GPU price normalization.
