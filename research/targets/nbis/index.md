## Reusable Facts

- Nebius Group N.V. is listed on Nasdaq under `NBIS` and headquartered in Amsterdam.
- Q1 2026 group revenue was $399.0M, up 684% year over year.
- Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was $129.5M at a 32% group adjusted EBITDA margin.
- The Nebius AI cloud business generated $174.0M of adjusted EBITDA in Q1 2026 at a 45% adjusted EBITDA margin.
- Q1 2026 property and equipment / intangible asset purchases were $2.4729B.
- Cash and cash equivalents were $9.2982B at 2026-03-31.
- Nebius reported $8.4320B of non-current debt at 2026-03-31.
- Nebius announced up to 1.2GW of power and land for a Pennsylvania AI factory in the Q1 2026 release.
- Nebius expects 800MW to 1GW of connected power by year-end 2026, according to its Q1 shareholder letter.
- Microsoft contract value is about $17.4B through 2031 and may rise to about $19.4B if Microsoft buys additional services/capacity.
- The March 2026 Meta agreement has potential contract value of up to about $27B over five years, split between $12B dedicated capacity and up to $15B additional available capacity.
- NVIDIA announced a $2B investment in Nebius and a strategic partnership that enables Nebius to deploy more than 5GW of NVIDIA systems by the end of 2030.
- Nebius is better understood as an NVIDIA ecosystem capacity operator than as a direct NVIDIA chip challenger.

## Open Questions

- Can Nebius bring enough powered, GPU-equipped capacity online on time to meet Microsoft and Meta service windows?
- How much of 2026-2027 growth is prepayment-funded versus debt-funded versus equity-funded?
- What are normalized gross margin, depreciation burden, and maintenance capex once the current GPU cohorts age?
- How diversified is revenue outside Microsoft, Meta, and large AI labs?
- Does Token Factory / Eigen AI / Clarifai / Tavily create real software differentiation, or mostly attach services around commodity GPU capacity?
- How much pricing pressure appears once Blackwell/Rubin supply normalizes and hyperscalers internalize more capacity?
- Does NVIDIA's 2026-05-20 Q1 FY2027 call change AI infrastructure sentiment or capacity economics for neocloud operators?
