## Practical Reading

The timeline supports four conclusions:

1. Terry's portfolio became more liquid and explicit over time: latest disclosed stocks/cash/crypto weights are clearer than older startup-equity valuation.
2. His private-company equity treatment became more conservative: older video counts it; latest video excludes it.
3. His yield framework matured: older Anchor-style high APY is historical context; newer NEXO videos emphasize source-of-yield and platform risk.
4. His ordinary-investor advice did not become "copy my concentration"; it became broad-market core, cash discipline, and advanced modules only with risk capacity.
