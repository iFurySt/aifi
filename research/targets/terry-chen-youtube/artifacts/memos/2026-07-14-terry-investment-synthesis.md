## 3. 普通人可迁移的投资组合方案

This is a practical template derived from Terry's path plus basic diversification/risk guardrails. It is a starting policy template, not a personal recommendation.

| Layer | Role | Practical starting range | Rule |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Runway / emergency liquidity | Cash, money market, short-duration safe instruments | 3-12 months expenses before risky assets | This prevents forced selling and gives career/business optionality. |
| Broad-market core | Low-cost diversified equity funds or target-date style allocation | 60-90% of risky assets | Default engine; every active bet must justify itself against this. |
| Stabilizer | Treasuries, high-quality bonds, cash equivalents | 0-40% depending on age, income stability, and drawdown tolerance | Do not copy Terry's low bond weight unless your risk capacity supports it. |
| Concentrated learning satellite | Single stocks with written thesis | 0-10% for most learners | One thesis, max size, failure rule, and review cadence. |
| Neutral / macro hedge sleeve | Gold, Bitcoin, commodities, or similar | 0-10%; often 0-5% for beginners | Only if you can explain why it diversifies and how it can fail. |
| Income overlay | Covered calls, cash-secured puts, covered-call ETFs | Paper trade first; small only | Not free yield; define assignment and missed-upside rules. |
| Operating assets | Rental, Airbnb, side business, creator/business projects | Case by case | Underwrite like a business; do not call gross revenue passive income. |
| Crypto/platform yield | BTC/ETH/custody/yield platforms | 0-5% for most learners | Separate asset volatility, custody risk, platform risk, stablecoin risk. |

For most ordinary investors, the simplest version is:

```text
Runway + broad ETF core + small learning satellite + written rules
```

The advanced version adds real estate, options, business income, or crypto yield only after the investor can model the failure cases.

## 4. Practice-First Learning Plan

This learning route is intentionally built from practice loops, not theory-first reading.

### Phase 1: Know The Personal Balance Sheet

- Track 90 days of income, expenses, taxes, debt, and savings.
- Calculate runway in months.
- Define the minimum emergency fund before investing aggressively.
- Pass condition: you know how much you can lose without changing your life.

### Phase 2: Build The Baseline Portfolio

- Create a mock broad-market portfolio.
- Compare lump-sum, monthly contribution, and drawdown scenarios.
- Learn rebalancing by writing target ranges.
- Pass condition: you can explain why broad-market indexing is the default and what would make you deviate.

Evidence anchor: Q59 supports broad-market investing as suitable for most people and frames active stock picking as behaviorally difficult (`Q59J5roE5lM.provider-youtubetranscript-pro.txt:259-331`).

Additional evidence anchor: the recovered war/market-panic short says uncertainty can make markets fall before events are fully realized, that Terry does not think he can predict the market bottom, and that long-term winners are investors who hold strong fundamentals through macro shocks rather than trying to sell everything and rebuy the exact low (`c24laHz3Vmo.provider-aivideosummarizer.txt:6-30`).

### Phase 3: One-Stock Research Drill

- Pick one company.
- Write bull case, bear case, valuation range, max size, and what would prove you wrong.
- Simulate a 30-50% drawdown before buying.
- Pass condition: the position size is small enough that you can follow the plan during stress.

### Phase 4: Income Overlay Lab

- Paper-trade 20 covered-call or cash-secured-put cycles.
- Track premium, assignment, roll decisions, taxes, and missed upside.
- Pass condition: you understand why option income is an obligation, not a coupon.

Evidence anchor: Terry discusses covered-call process, roll-up, earnings avoidance, and tools in `2avMoXe8Bwg.txt:41-46`.

### Phase 5: Real-Estate / Business Underwriting

- Build a rental spreadsheet with rent, vacancy, repairs, insurance, tax, manager fees, financing, legal structure, and exit value.
- For side business or creator work, track hours, gross revenue, net revenue, and stress.
- Pass condition: the project still works after conservative assumptions.

Evidence anchor: Terry's rental/Airbnb income is supported by delegated operations and management (`2avMoXe8Bwg.txt:51-59`; `Gwn_kEegfJ4.provider-aivideosummarizer.txt:79-117`).

### Phase 6: Crypto / Yield Risk Checklist

- Separate self-custody, exchange custody, stablecoin, lending platform, token price, and smart-contract risk.
- Write the maximum acceptable loss before chasing yield.
- Pass condition: you can explain where yield comes from and who loses money when stress happens.

Evidence anchor: the Nexo/yield videos discuss collateral, liquidation, and platform risk (`L7xgc12JfHA.provider-aivideosummarizer.txt:80-165`; `HL26tMSTqO4.provider-aivideosummarizer.txt:139-287`).

### Phase 7: Policy And Review Cadence

- Write target ranges by sleeve.
- Define rebalancing rules.
- Define max single-name exposure.
- Define what happens if job income, rental income, a platform, or a large holding fails.
- Review monthly for cash flow and quarterly for allocation.

This is the part that turns learning into behavior.

## What Not To Copy

- Do not copy the 47% stock / 19% real estate / 15% crypto / 19% cash mix unless your income stability, net worth, location, tax situation, and drawdown tolerance are similar.
- Do not copy Tesla-heavy concentration without the ability to hold through large drawdowns and the willingness to be wrong.
- Do not treat covered calls, QQQI, or crypto lending as cash.
- Do not treat Airbnb/rental revenue as passive without operations, management, and legal/liability planning.
- Do not treat creator income as salary replacement until the unit economics beat your core income engine.

## Practical Default

For a normal working person starting from scratch:

1. Keep 3-12 months runway.
2. Automate contributions to a diversified core.
3. Use a 0-10% satellite for learning, not identity.
4. Add one advanced module at a time: stock research, options, real estate, business, or crypto yield.
5. Write rules before scaling.
6. Measure progress by resilience and investable surplus, not by copying someone else's holdings.
