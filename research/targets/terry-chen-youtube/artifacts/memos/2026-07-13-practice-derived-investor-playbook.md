## Ordinary-Investor Portfolio Template

This is a default template for research and self-assessment, not a recommendation for a specific person.

| Layer | Suggested role | Starting range | Why it exists | Evidence / guardrail |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Runway and liquidity | Cash, emergency fund, short-duration safe yield | 3-12 months expenses before risky assets | Avoid forced selling and preserve career/business optionality. | Terry failover evidence; FINRA risk basics. |
| Broad-market core | Low-cost diversified equity funds or target-date style allocation | 60-90% of risky assets | The default engine for compounding and diversification. | Q59 ordinary-investor evidence; FINRA diversification; Investor.gov compounding. |
| Bond/cash-like stabilizer | Treasuries, high-quality bond funds, cash equivalents | 0-40% based on time horizon and drawdown tolerance | Reduce sequence and liquidity pressure. | Terry IB01/short-term Treasury references; FINRA asset allocation. |
| Neutral-asset diversifier | Gold, Bitcoin, commodity exposure, or similar macro hedges | 0-10% for most learners, only with clear risk notes | Stress-tests the classic stock/bond model against inflation, currency, and geopolitical reserve risk. | XJtbWhnclB4 60/40 critique; FINRA risk basics. |
| Concentrated satellite | Single stocks such as tech names only with written thesis | 0-10%, advanced investors maybe 10-20% | Lets high-conviction research matter without letting one thesis dominate life outcomes. | Terry concentration evidence; FINRA concentration-risk warning. |
| Income overlay | Covered calls only on owned positions | 0-10% of portfolio, or paper-trade first | Income tool, but with missed-upside and assignment risk. | Terry covered-call process evidence. |
| Operating assets | Rental, Airbnb, consulting, side business | Case-by-case, not forced | Can diversify income, but requires operations and liability control. | Terry Airbnb/business-income evidence. |
| Crypto/yield sleeve | Bitcoin/crypto/stablecoin lending only if custody and platform risk are understood | 0-5% for most learners | Optional asymmetric/risk sleeve, never core living capital. | Terry crypto-yield evidence; risk guardrails. |

The simplest ordinary version is: cash runway first, then a broad diversified core, then one small learning satellite. Terry's full model is more complex because it includes high income, business operations, options, real estate, and crypto/yield products.

## Practice-First Learning Plan

| Stage | Practice task | Pass condition | What not to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Cash-flow map | Track 90 days of income, expenses, debt, taxes, and runway. | Know monthly burn, savings rate, and minimum emergency fund. | Do not start with options or crypto yield. |
| 2. Index baseline | Build a mock broad-market portfolio and compare it with cash over 1, 3, 5, and 10-year scenarios. | Can explain why diversification lowers single-name damage. | Do not assume stock picking is required to build wealth. |
| 3. One-stock thesis | Pick one company, write bull case, bear case, valuation, max size, and sell rule. | Can predefine what would prove the thesis wrong. | Do not buy more only because price fell. |
| 4. Drawdown drill | Simulate a 30-50% drawdown in the satellite sleeve. | Know whether you would buy, hold, trim, or stop. | Do not claim volatility tolerance without sizing it. |
| 5. Covered-call paper run | Paper trade 20 covered-call cycles on a position you would be willing to own or trim. | Can explain assignment, roll, tax, and missed-upside outcomes. | Do not sell calls on positions you cannot afford to lose upside on. |
| 6. Rental/business underwriting | Build a spreadsheet for vacancy, repairs, taxes, insurance, management, financing, and legal structure. | Cash flow remains positive under conservative assumptions. | Do not treat gross rent or Airbnb revenue as net profit. |
| 7. Crypto custody and platform-risk checklist | Separate self-custody, exchange, lending platform, stablecoin, and token-price risks. | Can define maximum loss if a platform fails. | Do not call platform yield cash. |
| 8. Written portfolio policy | Write target ranges, rebalance rules, max single-name size, and income-sleeve limits. | Rules can be followed during stress without improvising. | Do not keep strategy only in memory. |
| 9. Side-income unit economics | Track hours, direct costs, gross revenue, net revenue, and stress level for creator/business projects. | Know whether the side income is skill-building, distribution, optional upside, or real salary replacement. | Do not quit the core income engine because gross revenue looks exciting. |
| 10. Macro allocation stress test | Compare a basic 60/40, equity-heavy, and neutral-asset sleeve portfolio under inflation and stock/bond double-drawdown scenarios. | Can explain why each sleeve exists and what could make it fail. | Do not add gold/Bitcoin/commodities only because they recently outperformed. |
