## Reusable Facts

- Anthropic is privately held and not directly listed.
- Anthropic announced a $30B Series G at a $380B post-money valuation.
- Anthropic stated annualized run-rate revenue was $14B in February 2026 and later surpassed $30B in April 2026.
- Anthropic stated Claude Code exceeded $2.5B annualized run-rate revenue.
- Anthropic highlighted more than 500,000 enterprise customers and more than 80% of Fortune 500 companies using Claude.
- Amazon and Anthropic expanded their AWS-centered strategic collaboration, including up to 5GW of capacity, more than $100B of ten-year AWS technology commitments, and Project Rainier context.
- Anthropic announced a SpaceX compute partnership for more than 300MW of Colossus 1 capacity and over 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs within the month.
- Anthropic acquired Stainless, the SDK and MCP server tooling company behind official Anthropic SDK generation.
- Anthropic and KPMG announced a global alliance to embed Claude in KPMG Digital Gateway, provide Claude access to KPMG's 276,000+ global workforce, and make KPMG a preferred partner for private equity.
- Anthropic / Claude launched Claude for the legal industry with 20+ MCP connectors and 12 legal-work plugins.
- U.S. government-access friction around Anthropic's AI-use restrictions remains a policy and litigation watch item.
- Anthropic said Project Glasswing and about 50 partners used Claude Mythos Preview to find more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities across systemically important software.
- Anthropic said Claude Security is in public beta for Claude Enterprise customers and that Claude Opus 4.7 had been used to patch more than 2,100 vulnerabilities in three weeks.
