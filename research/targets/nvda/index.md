## Reusable Facts

- Q4 FY2026 revenue was $68.127B, up 20% QoQ and 73% YoY.
- FY2026 revenue was $215.938B, up 65% YoY.
- FY2026 Data Center revenue was $193.7B, up 68% YoY.
- Q4 FY2026 Data Center revenue was $62.3B, up 22% QoQ and 75% YoY.
- FY2026 GAAP net income was $120.067B and diluted EPS was $4.90.
- Q1 FY2027 revenue guidance was $78.0B plus or minus 2%; gross margin guide was about 75%.
- NVIDIA did not assume any China Data Center compute revenue in Q1 FY2027 guidance.
- FY2026 operating cash flow was $102.718B; cash plus marketable securities were $62.556B.
- Two direct customers represented 22% and 14% of FY2026 revenue.
- Inventory purchase and long-term supply/capacity obligations were $95.2B as of 2026-01-25.
- Next scheduled earnings call: 2026-05-20.
- Q1 FY2027 results are scheduled for approximately 2026-05-20 1:20 p.m. PT, with the call at 2:00 p.m. PT; in China this is early 2026-05-21.
