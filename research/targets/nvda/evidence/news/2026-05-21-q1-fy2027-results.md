## Baseline Comparison

- Prior archive state only tracked the event timing and checklist; actual Q1
  results were not available in the 2026-05-20 evidence note.
- Q1 revenue exceeded the $78B guide midpoint by about $3.6B.
- The Q2 revenue guide of $91B, if achieved at midpoint, would represent about
  11.5% sequential growth from Q1 FY2027.
- Gross margin remains close to the 75% guide, so the result does not currently
  support the bear case that Blackwell / Blackwell Ultra system transitions are
  sharply diluting profitability.
- China is still an unresolved upside / risk variable: management again excludes
  China Data Center compute revenue from the forward outlook, so the core beat
  does not require a China recovery.

## Investment-Relevant Interpretation

- This is alert-worthy. The result resolved the prior catalyst and was stronger
  than the tracked baseline on both Q1 actuals and Q2 guide.
- The highest-quality element is not just the Q1 beat; it is the $91B Q2 guide
  while excluding China Data Center compute revenue.
- The main risk after this print is valuation / expectation reset rather than
  immediate fundamental deterioration. Investors may now price in continued
  double-digit sequential growth, making any future moderation more punitive.
