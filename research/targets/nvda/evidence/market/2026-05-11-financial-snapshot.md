## Financial Interpretation

- NVIDIA is not just growing fast; it is compounding revenue at hyperscale while retaining software-like margins.
- The highest-quality part of the story is Data Center: $193.7B full-year revenue and $62.3B Q4 revenue make gaming a much smaller part of the investment case.
- Margin pressure is understandable, not fatal: FY2026 gross margin fell mainly because of H20 charges and the transition from Hopper HGX to full-scale Blackwell data center systems.
- The balance sheet is a strategic asset. $62.6B of cash and marketable securities and $102.7B of operating cash flow give NVIDIA room to prepay capacity, invest in partners, and repurchase stock.
- The same strength creates a risk: $95.2B of supply obligations means the company is underwriting future AI demand far in advance.
