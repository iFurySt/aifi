## Investment-Relevant Interpretation

- The market signal is not bearish. It is a warning that the stock has already
  absorbed a great deal of good news.
- The operating result strengthened the bull case, while the muted after-hours
  reaction strengthened the valuation-discipline point from the prior thesis
  frame.
- The best evidence for durability is the Q2 guide excluding China Data Center
  compute revenue. If China remains excluded and revenue still compounds, the
  core AI factory demand story is broader than export-policy upside.
- The main missing piece remains the Q1 FY2027 10-Q. It should be checked for
  updated supply obligations, inventory/prepayment movement, customer
  concentration, and any reporting-framework detail that validates the
  Hyperscale / ACIE split.
